# About me:

**Full Professor of Computer Engineering | Artificial Intelligence Researcher | Visiting Researcher at InfAI Dresden**

I am a Full Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at **Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman, Iran**, and currently a Visiting Researcher at the **Institute for Applied Computer Science (InfAI), Dresden, Germany**.

I received my **B.Sc. in Computer Engineering** from Shiraz University in 2001, followed by **M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in Artificial Intelligence** from the same university in 2004 and 2008, respectively. I have been a faculty member at Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman since 2008.

My research focuses on **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Fuzzy Systems, Foundation Models, Large Language Models, and Intelligent Information Systems**. I have authored or co-authored approximately **180 research papers** in international journals and conference proceedings.
